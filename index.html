How accurate is the current-round prediction? (live, logged by this tool)

Each round, the tool records its call at 1:00, 2:00, 3:00 and 4:00 into the round and checks it against the Binance 5-minute result. "Profit per $1" assumes you bought the predicted side at the Predict.fun price shown at that moment and held to the end (fees and slippage not included). Later checkpoints are more accurate but the price is higher, so the profit per win is smaller.

Model check on the last 2 days (thousands of rounds)

When the raw model says e.g. 80–90%, this is how often it was really right. The tool uses the real number for "most to pay", so it never overpays for an over-confident call.
Read this first
• No tool wins every round. A late call is right more often, but the side is already expensive: buying at 85¢ wins about $0.18 per $1 and a loss costs the full $1, so you need to be right about 85% of the time just to break even.
• The suggestion only says BUY when the market price is below what the model thinks the side is worth (with a 3-point safety margin). Otherwise it says skip – skipping a bad price is how you keep small profits.
• The next round is close to a coin flip before it starts; the lean shown is weak (see its historical accuracy).
• Predict.fun settles on Chainlink's price, the tool uses Binance – very close, but near-flat rounds can differ.
